## Authority
Every new project, including competitive upgrades, must receive explicit authenticated owner approval of its exact proposal revision before execution. Research and non-committing internal proposal preparation may occur within existing resource limits. Owner may approve, reject, amend, suspend or revoke. Routine tasks within an approved project may be delegated only within its explicit scope; any expansion, new project, new jurisdiction, material agreement, capital increase or pricing change requires fresh approval. No agent can approve itself, expand authority, modify signing keys, weaken policy or bypass an independent gate. Existing obligations must be safely discharged after suspension. Essential security, reliability and legal maintenance has a separate protective lane, not a blanket approval exemption for new projects.
