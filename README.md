TrillionAI Manager

TrillionAI Manager is a business operations and management platform designed to help organizations coordinate customers, services, workflows, projects, and day-to-day operations from one central system.

Overview

The platform is being developed to simplify business management through intelligent automation, streamlined workflows, and a unified operational dashboard.

Its purpose is to reduce repetitive administration, improve visibility across business activities, and help teams manage work more efficiently.

Planned Capabilities

TrillionAI Manager is intended to support areas such as:

* customer management
* lead and enquiry tracking
* quotes and proposals
* project coordination
* workflow automation
* subscription and service management
* reporting and analytics
* operational dashboards
* notifications and alerts
* business process automation
* integrations with external services
* AI-assisted business operations

Goals

The project is focused on creating a platform that is:

* easy to use
* scalable
* secure
* modular
* automation-first
* suitable for real commercial use
* capable of supporting growing businesses

Technology

The application is being developed using a modern cross-platform architecture.

Primary technologies include:

* Flutter
* Dart
* Node.js
* TypeScript
* PostgreSQL
* modern cloud infrastructure

Development Status

Status: In Development

Features, integrations, architecture, and functionality may change as the product evolves.
